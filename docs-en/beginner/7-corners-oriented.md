## The absolute rule

!!! danger "NEVER turn the whole cube during this step"
    It is the one way to destroy everything three moves from the end.

    To move from one corner to the next, you turn **only the top face** with
    `U`. The cube itself stays still in your hands: the corner you are working
    on is **always top right, in front of you**.

<div class="objectif" markdown>
![3d-coin-tourne](../assets/cubes/3d-coin-tourne.svg)
<figcaption>The corner is already in the right spot: the sequence only twists it on itself.</figcaption>
</div>

## The procedure

1. Bring an **unsolved** corner to the top right, in front of you, with `U`.
2. Do `R' D' R D` until its yellow is on top (2 or 4 times).
3. With `U` **and nothing else**, bring the next unsolved corner to that same
   spot.
4. Go back to point 2.

When the last corner is oriented, the top layer falls back into place by itself
on the final `U`. The cube is solved.


!!! warning "The scary moment"
    Between the second and third corner, the cube looks **completely
    destroyed**: the bottom is broken, the colours are all over the place.

<div class="objectif" markdown>
![cube casse](../assets/cubes/cube-casse.svg)
<figcaption>Half way through step 7, the cube looks like this. <b>Nothing is broken</b>: as long as you only did <code>U</code> moves between repetitions, it all falls back into place.</figcaption>
</div>

    That is normal, and it is the point where most people give up thinking they
    made a mistake. **Keep going.** As long as you only did `U` moves between
    repetitions, everything falls back into place at the end. Trust yourself.
