The **41 cases** for inserting a corner + edge pair into the front-right slot.

!!! warning "Read this before diving in"
    Learning these 41 cases by heart is **the worst possible route**. F2L is
    learned [intuitively](../cfop/f2l.md) first: you understand how to pull the
    pair out, join it, and put it away. This page is a *reference*, to look up
    when one particular case keeps costing you time.

Every algorithm below is the **shortest possible** one in `R`, `U` and `F`: they
were found by exhaustive search, not copied. The greyed-out pieces do not matter
for the case.
