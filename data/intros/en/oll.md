The **57 cases of Orientation of the Last Layer**. You hold the cube with the
yellow face on top; the algorithm turns that face entirely yellow without
disturbing the first two layers.

!!! tip "You don't need all 57 at once"
    Start with the **7 cases in the first section**: combined with the yellow
    cross, they are enough to orient any last layer in two steps. That is the
    method explained in [The last layer in 4 looks](../cfop/4lll.md).

The diagram shows the top face seen from above; the small tabs around it are the
yellow stickers visible **on the sides**. Grey is not a colour: it means
"anything but yellow".
